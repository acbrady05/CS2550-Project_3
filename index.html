What is the Cyber Branch

The Army Cyber Branch (Branch 17) is the Army's career field for officers who plan and conduct operations in cyberspace, and it became a basic branch in 2014 after years of cyber functions being spread across other specialties like Signal and Military Intelligence. Its officers (Cyber Operations Officers, designated 17A) and warrant officers (such as 170A Cyber Operations Technician) lead teams that carry out defensive cyberspace operations to protect Army and Department of Defense networks, offensive cyberspace operations against adversary systems, and DoD Information Network operations. They serve primarily in Army Cyber Command (ARCYBER), in Cyber Protection Teams, and in joint organizations like U.S. Cyber Command, often working alongside intelligence and electronic warfare personnel. Enlisted Soldiers fill the related Cyber Operations Specialist role (MOS 17C), and the branch's training pipeline runs through Fort Gordon, Georgia, which has been the Army's cyber center of excellence. Because the work overlaps with intelligence, signals, and information operations, it's a natural point of comparison for anyone in Military Intelligence who's curious about where those skills lead.

History of the Branch

Oct 2010

The Army established ARCYBER as it's component to U.S. Cyber Command (USCYBERCOM) and it's integrator for cyberspace operations

May 2014

Seven cadets at the U.S. Military Academy were commissioned into Signal or Military Intelligence, with the plan to transfer to Cyber once the branch existed.

2014

General Order 2014-2 made the ARCYBER commanding general also the commanding general of Sccond Army, and assigned the Network Enterprise Technology Command (NETCOM) to Second Army. The command also cerated Regional Cyber Centers to improve global network defense.

1 Sep 2014

General Orders 2014-63, signed by Secretary of the Army John McHugh, Established the branch.It created a career path for Soldiers, warrant officers, and officers, and the 17-series MOS was finalized. Army Training and Doctrine Command had advocated for a Cyber school and career field.

Nov 2014

Two months after the branch was created, 15 west point cadets became the first class to branch directly into Cyber.

2015

Distinctive insignia was authorized for officers and enlisted personnel in the branch.

2019

The 915th Cyber Warfare Battalion was officially created.It fields tactical cyber teams.

1 Sep 2024

The branch's 10th anniversary. It is still the Army's youngest career branch, placed in the Operations functional category.

Fun Fact of the Day (Never changes)

The first computer worm to spread across the internet, the Morris Worm of 1988, was written by a cornell grad student who said he meant it to gauge the size of the internet, not to cause harm. A bug made it re-infect machines slowing an estimated 10 percent of the roughly 60,000 computers online at the time to a crawl.
